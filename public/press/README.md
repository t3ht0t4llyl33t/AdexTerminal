# aDEX Terminal — Media Kit

Assets for wallet directories, catalog listings, press coverage and partner materials.

## Brand assets (drop-in files)

- `/logo.png` — 512×512 primary logo with wordmark and network badges. Use for share cards, Telegram previews, and directory listings.
- `/icon.svg` — Scalable primary icon (same composition as `logo.png` without the fine text). Use for favicons, TonConnect connection sheets and any place that needs a resolution-independent mark.

Both files live at the site root so they are trivially quotable:
- `https://adexterminal.com/logo.png`
- `https://adexterminal.com/icon.svg`

## Screenshots

Place mobile Mini App captures here as `screen-radar-en.png`, `screen-whales-en.png`, `screen-scanner-en.png` (and matching `-ru.png` versions). Recommended resolution: 1170×2532 (iPhone 14 Pro) at 72 dpi.

## Short description (≤ 160 chars)

Telegram-native DEX radar for TON, BSC and Base — spot volume spikes, follow whale wallets and scan contract safety in one tap.

## Long description (≤ 500 chars)

aDEX Terminal is a Telegram Mini App that turns crypto research into a single tap. A live Volume Radar tracks fresh momentum across TON, BSC and Base; a Whale X-Ray surfaces the wallets that move markets; a Contract Vault runs safety scans powered by GoPlus signals. TON Connect handles payments, referrals reward on-chain, and Telegram alerts deliver every important signal straight to your chat.
